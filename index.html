Our Story

Welcome to Pinch of Spice Kitchen and Grill, where culinary passion meets tradition. Located in the heart of Mangaon, Maharashtra, we've been serving the community with authentic Indian cuisine that brings together the best of traditional flavors and modern cooking techniques.

What started as a dream to serve delicious, quality food has grown into a beloved local restaurant. Every dish we prepare is a testament to our commitment to excellence, using only the freshest ingredients and time-honored recipes.

From our signature Chinese rice dishes to our mouth-watering tandoor specialties, each plate is crafted with love, care, and a generous pinch of spice that makes our food truly special.

Meet Our Founder

"Food is not just about taste - it's about creating memories and bringing people together. At Pinch of Spice, every dish tells a story."

With a passion for authentic Indian cuisine and years of culinary expertise, our founder established Pinch of Spice to bring the finest flavors to Mangaon. We believe in quality over quantity, authenticity over shortcuts, and customer satisfaction above all.

Our team is dedicated to ensuring that every meal you enjoy is prepared with the same care and attention we'd give to our own family. Whether it's a quick lunch or a special celebration, we're honored to be part of your dining experience.
